## Description
Inside a padlock, five spring-loaded pin stacks block the plug. A wrong key lifts them to the wrong heights. The right key lifts every pin to exactly the gap between plug and body, and the plug turns.
Simplified 3D visualization, not real footage.

## Sources
- US 9,809,995 and related "High-strength lock" patents: pin pairs (driver pin on a tumbler/key pin) with springs; only the correct key positions the pins so the plug can turn.
- US 8,082,766 "Pin tumbler lock releasing system": the plug and casing meet along the shear line; the right key brings every key-pin top to that line.
- US 10,100,553: key pins of various lengths, driver pins pushed down by springs; a wrong key leaves pins straddling the shear point.

Notes: simplified single-sided pin layout (real locks have pins on one side of the plug too, with a keyway profile that is not shown). The shackle release (the plug turning and freeing the shackle) is shown schematically. No brand or model name appears.
