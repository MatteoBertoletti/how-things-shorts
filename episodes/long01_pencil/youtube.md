## Description
There is no lead in your pencil, and there never was. The grey line is graphite, a form of carbon. In under five minutes: why it's called lead, what graphite really is, how the core is made from graphite and clay and baked in a kiln at about 1,100 °C, how two grooved cedar slats are glued around the cores, and how a pencil gets its paint, metal ferrule and eraser.

Simplified 3D visualization and diagrams, not real factory footage. Kiln temperatures in sources range from about 980 °C to 1,100 °C, so the video says "about".
