## Script (83 words, Brian, same settings) — estimated ~31.7 s with a 2.5 s end card
1. Why do toy bricks stick? There's no glue.
2. The stud is slightly too big for the gap.
3. So the plastic bends, and squeezes back.
4. The molds are accurate to a hundredth of a millimetre. [0.01 MM]
5. It starts as plastic grains, melted at about two hundred thirty degrees Celsius. [ABOUT 230 °C]
6. Injected into a steel mold, with up to a hundred and fifty tons of pressure. [UP TO 150 TONS]
7. About seven seconds later, a brick drops out. [ABOUT 7 SECONDS]
8. Press two together. Squeeze, not glue.
9. Gummy bear, or ballpoint pen? Comment below.
