## Description
No glue, no click: the stud is slightly too big for the gap, so the plastic bends and squeezes back. Then a brick is born from plastic grains in about seven seconds.
Simplified 3D visualization, not real footage. The bending in the cutaway is exaggerated.

Cooling: about seven seconds. The real bending is microscopic; the cutaway exaggerates it and says so on screen.
